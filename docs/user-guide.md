# Using Nubi

A walk-through of the map and the assistant at [nubi.yonsei.ac.kr](https://nubi.yonsei.ac.kr). The site is in
Korean; the labels below are given as they appear on screen, with their meaning.

## Signing in

1. Open [nubi.yonsei.ac.kr/signup](https://nubi.yonsei.ac.kr/signup), agree to the terms and sign up with Google.
   This is needed once.
2. **Yonsei accounts** (`@yonsei.ac.kr`) are admitted at once. **Other Google accounts** fill in the 이용 신청
   (application) form: name, affiliation and purpose. Access starts when the maintainer approves the request.
3. Afterwards sign in at [nubi.yonsei.ac.kr](https://nubi.yonsei.ac.kr). A sign-in lasts 7 days.

## The map

The map is the main page. The campus loads once (a few seconds on the first visit) and is drawn in your browser;
a computer or recent phone with WebGL is enough. The drawing quality adapts to your graphics hardware.

| Control | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **3인칭 / 1인칭** | top left | third person (look over the campus) or first person (walk at eye level) |
| **정문으로** | top left | back to the main gate |
| **지하** | top left | the underground view |
| **?** | top left | the controls for the current view |
| time bar (sun icon) | top right | the time of day; the sun is the real sun over Sinchon for today's date |
| **출처** | bottom corner | the data sources and their licences |

Walls stop you and you slide along them; stairs, ramps and slopes follow the ground.

## Finding a place

The box in the middle of the screen (건물·장소를 찾거나 물어보세요, "find a building or place, or ask") searches as
you type: buildings by name, English name, short name or building number, halls, outdoor places, paths and
facilities. Pick a result and the map flies there and opens its card: name, number, storeys and height, the year it was
built, its entrances and the facilities inside, with their hours.

## Routes (길찾기)

On a place card press **여기까지 길찾기** (route here) or **여기서 출발** (start here), then choose the other end. The
route card offers three profiles:

| Profile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **최단** | the quickest walk, slopes included |
| **계단 없이** | no steps anywhere on the way (wheelchairs, strollers, luggage) |
| **오르막 적게** | the least climbing |

The card gives the walking time, distance, metres climbed, number of steps and the way in words ("백양로를 따라 …"). The
line on the map stays visible through buildings. Routes use building entrances, crossings and the underground passages
under 백양로 when they are shorter.

## Underground (지하)

**지하** turns the ground and the buildings to glass. Choose a level (B1 to B7) to see each building's basement at that
level, coloured by its use (parking, food, lecture rooms, research, plant rooms …), the floors and halls of 백양누리,
and the underground passages. Click a level or a hall for its card; every card has 여기까지 길찾기. Some uses are
listed for a level without an exact position; the card says so.

## Time and shade

Drag the time bar to any minute of the day: the sun, the light and the shadows follow. At night the windows and lamps
light up. Ask the assistant for another date, a sunset, or how much of a place is in shade.

## The assistant

Type a question instead of a name and press Enter (or pick "… 물어보기" in the list). The assistant answers in Korean
and acts on the map: it flies to places, draws routes, pins facilities, moves the clock or plays a tour. Examples:

- 첨단관에서 백양누리 가는 길 알려줘
- 학생회관에서 중앙도서관, 계단 없이
- 지금 열린 카페 어디 있어?
- 정문 근처 한식집, 걸어서 5분 안
- 노천극장 오후 3시에 그늘 얼마나 돼?
- 20분짜리 캠퍼스 투어 보여줘
- 백양누리 지하 2층에 뭐가 있어?
- 지하에 주차장이 있는 건물은?

Answers come only from Nubi's data; when the data does not know (for example, the hours of a place that does not
publish them), the assistant says so. Your conversations are listed under 지난 대화 (past conversations), and you can
delete them. Each person has a number of questions per day.

## Your account

The account menu (top right) opens 내 계정 (my account): your sign-in, its expiry and your sessions on other devices.
You can sign out everywhere or delete the account.

## Good to know

- Nubi is an independent personal project, not an official Yonsei University service. For official information
  (schedules, booking, notices) follow the links on the cards to the university's pages.
- Opening hours change with the semester and vacations; each facility card shows the page and the day its hours
  were read.
- The map is built from public data and checked by hand, but it can be wrong. Small buildings, gates and new
  construction are the most likely places.
